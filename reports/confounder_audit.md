# Confounder Audit & Identification Strategy (Day 4)

Causal Inference for Marketing Attribution — written **before fitting** (AGENTS.md §3).

## 1. Identification strategy (per channel)

### Channel: `email`  (embedded true effect log-odds = +0.12 — reactivation works)

* **Causal question:** what is the average causal effect of exposure to `sim_exposed_email` on 14-day conversion and follow-on revenue for the 94,983-customer Olist cohort?
* **Treatment:** `sim_exposed_email` (0/1).
* **Outcomes:** `sim_converted_14d` (primary), `sim_revenue_14d` (secondary).
* **Sufficient observed adjustment set:** `order_count`, `recency_days`, `review_score_avg`, `total_revenue` (common causes of assignment and outcome in the preview DGP).
* **Why assignment is confounded:** the targeting rule scores customers on those features (plus latent `sim_u`), so exposed customers already differ in baseline intent — naive exposure-vs-control comparisons are biased.

## 2. Assumptions checklist (evidence & limitation)

| Assumption | Evidence | Status | Limitation |
|------------|----------|--------|------------|
| Exchangeability given the adjustment set | Targeting rule is a deterministic function of the observed features + `sim_u`; conditioning on the observed set closes all observed backdoor paths | **Violated by design** (residual `sim_u` confounding) | Sensitivity analysis (Day 18) quantifies how strong U must be to explain results |
| Positivity / overlap | Exposure rates 26-37% per channel (Day-3 preview); propensity score must avoid extremes | To verify (Day 6 overlap diagnostics) | Customers deterministic in targeting (impossible scores) would break it |
| Consistency | Exposure flag is well-defined (single campaign snapshot, no dose variants) | Plausible in preview | Full campaign grid (Week 2) adds timing variants -> re-check |
| SUTVA / no interference | One customer's exposure does not affect another's outcome in the simulation (no peer effects wired) | Plausible in the sim | Real word-of-mouth spillover would violate; per-channel analysis limits the joint multi-channel issue |
